TRADE HUB BANNER IMAGES
=======================

Drop these 4 image files in this folder (public/images/) to show them
as banner backgrounds on the Trade Hub headers. Recommended size:
800x400px or wider, JPG format.

1. tradehub-sendgold.jpg   - Latest Standoff2 cover art
2. tradehub-swaps.jpg      - Collage of games whose tokens can be swapped
                             (Standoff2, Roblox, Fortnite, PUBG, Valorant, Steam...)
3. tradehub-skins.jpg      - CS2 inventory / skins collage
4. tradehub-giftcards.jpg  - Collage of gift cards & accounts sold on the platform
                             (Steam, Google Play, PSN, Xbox cards + game accounts)

Until the images are added, each banner shows a themed gradient fallback,
so the page always looks good.
