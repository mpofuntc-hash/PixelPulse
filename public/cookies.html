Cookie Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us recognize your browser, remember your preferences, and improve your experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

PixelPulse uses cookies for the following purposes:

Cookie TypePurposeExamples
EssentialRequired for the Platform to function.Session token, login state, currency preference.
FunctionalImprove usability and remember your settings.Theme, display mode, chat nickname.
AnalyticsHelp us understand how users interact with the Platform.Visitor ID, page views, session duration.
AdvertisingMeasure ad campaign effectiveness and prevent duplicate conversions.UTM source, click ID, conversion events.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party advertising and analytics partners. These partners may set their own cookies in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not control third-party cookies.

4. Managing Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View stored cookies.
  • Delete individual or all cookies.
  • Block cookies from specific websites.
  • Block all cookies (may break some features).

Disabling essential cookies will prevent you from logging in or using core features.

5. Cookie Consent

By continuing to use the Platform, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy. We may display a cookie banner for new visitors to confirm consent where required by local law.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page.
