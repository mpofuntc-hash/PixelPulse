## Notes Before Sending (read this, don't send it)

1. **Fill in the placeholders** — Discord username, Telegram handle, web app URL, date.
2. **Be honest about gambling** — Tradeit works adjacent to the gambling space
   (skin trading feeds many gambling platforms), but if they ask directly whether 
   PixelPulse is real-money gaming, say yes. Getting caught misrepresenting kills 
   the deal permanently.
3. **Expect a licensing question** — major platforms may ask if you're licensed. 
   Have an answer ready. If unlicensed, emphasize provably fair mechanics, the 
   collateralized pool, and your compliance roadmap.
4. **Don't message multiple admins at once** — pick one (ideally someone with a 
   "Partnerships" or "Business" role), and wait 3–5 days before a polite follow-up.
5. **If they say no** — ask if they have an affiliate program you can join anyway. 
   That's a "yes" you can get without a partnership.
6. **Realistic expectations** — Tradeit is major; a full partnership with an 
   unlaunched platform is a long shot. The affiliate route (Tier 1) is the 
   realistic win here. Frame everything so Tier 1 is easy to say yes to.
