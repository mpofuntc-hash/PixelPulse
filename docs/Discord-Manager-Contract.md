## 1. PURPOSE

The Company engages the Manager to grow and manage the PixelPulse Discord community and drive new user registrations to the platform at `https://pixelpulse.zentriva-clubsync.online/` through organic community engagement, content creation, and referral-based user acquisition.

---

## 2. TERM

- **Start Date:** [Start Date]
- **Initial Term:** 3 months
- **Renewal:** Automatic month-to-month renewal unless either party gives 14 days written notice (Discord DM or email counts as written notice)
- Either party may terminate immediately for breach of contract

---

## 3. SCOPE OF WORK

The Manager shall:

1. **Discord Server Management**
   - Set up and maintain the PixelPulse Discord server (channels, roles, bots, rules)
   - Moderate daily, enforce community guidelines, remove spam/toxic users
   - Keep the server active with daily engagement (announcements, polls, discussions)

2. **User Acquisition**
   - Promote the platform using a **unique referral link** assigned to the Manager
   - Share the referral link in relevant gaming communities, forums, and social media (within each platform's rules — no spam)
   - All users who register via the Manager's referral link are tracked as "Referred Users"

3. **Content Creation**
   - Create weekly content: clips highlights, prediction discussions, marketplace spotlights
   - Post platform updates and announcements to the Discord community

4. **Reporting**
   - Provide a monthly summary (last day of each month) of:
     - Total Discord members
     - New members that month
     - Referred Users that month
     - Active engagement metrics (messages, reactions, event participation)

## 4. COMPENSATION

### 4.1 No Base Salary

The parties acknowledge that the Company currently has **no budget for a fixed base salary**. Compensation is **entirely performance-based** and tied to measurable results.

### 4.2 Monthly Performance Payment

The Manager shall be paid monthly based on the following tiered structure:

| **Monthly Active Referred Users (MARU)** | **Monthly Payment (USD)** |
|---|---|
| 0 – 9 | $0 |
| 10 – 24 | $25 |
| 25 – 49 | $50 |
| 50 – 99 | $100 |
| 100 – 199 | $175 |
| 200 – 399 | $300 |
| 400 – 799 | $500 |
| 800 – 1,499 | $750 |
| 1,500 – 2,999 | $1,000 |
| 3,000+ | $1,200 (capped) |

**Definitions:**
- **Monthly Active Referred User (MARU):** A user who (a) registered via the Manager's unique referral link, (b) logged in at least once during the calendar month, and (c) completed at least one action (viewed a clip, placed a prediction, listed/bought a skin, sent a chat message, or made a wallet deposit).
- MARU count is determined by the Company's internal database and analytics. The Company will share the monthly report with the Manager.

### 4.3 Revenue Share Bonus (Optional — Activated When Site Generates Revenue)

Once the platform's gross monthly revenue exceeds **$5,000 USD**, the Manager becomes eligible for an additional bonus:

- **2% of gross monthly revenue**, capped at **$2,000 USD per month**
- This bonus is in addition to the tiered MARU payment above
- Gross revenue includes: marketplace transaction fees, betting fees, token conversion fees, and any other platform revenue
- This bonus replaces the MARU tiered payment once monthly gross revenue exceeds **$50,000 USD** (at which point the Manager receives 2% of gross revenue, capped at $5,000/month)

### 4.4 Payment Terms

- Payments are made within **7 business days** after the end of each calendar month
- Payment method: **BTC** (to a wallet address provided by the Manager) or **bank transfer** (if available in the Manager's country)
- The Company will provide a monthly statement showing MARU count, revenue (if applicable), and payment calculation

### 4.5 Cap on Total Monthly Compensation

Regardless of performance, the Manager's **total monthly compensation shall not exceed $5,000 USD** (combining MARU payment + revenue share bonus). This cap protects the Company during periods of rapid growth and ensures sustainable operations.
